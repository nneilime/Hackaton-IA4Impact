# Nordlys Bikes — Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you are free
to organise your day, as long as you work 37.5 hours per week on average.

## Remote work

You can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday is the shared office day: every
team is expected on site so that meetings and workshops can happen in person.
Working from abroad is allowed for up to 20 days per year with your manager's approval.

## Vacation

- Every employee gets 27 vacation days per year, plus public holidays.
- Request vacation at least 2 weeks in advance in the HR portal ("Fjord").
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire
  on March 31.
- The office is closed between Christmas and New Year; those days are not deducted.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager before 09:00 on the first day you are sick. A doctor's note is
required from the 4th consecutive sick day.

## Expenses

- Meals while travelling: up to €45 per day.
- Prefer the train over the plane for trips under 600 km.
- Submit receipts in Fjord within 30 days, otherwise they cannot be reimbursed.

## Equipment

New employees choose their laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad X1). There is a one-time
home office budget of €300 for a chair, desk or screen. Every employee also gets a
Nordlys E1 bike on loan for as long as they work here.
