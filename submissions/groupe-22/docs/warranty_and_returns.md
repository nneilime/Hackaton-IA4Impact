# Warranty and Returns Policy

## 30-day returns

You can return your bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if:

- it has been ridden less than 50 km,
- it is undamaged,
- it is sent back in the original packaging.

Return shipping is free within the EU. From the UK, Switzerland and Norway, return
shipping costs €89.

## Warranty

| Part | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Frame | 10 years |
| Motor and electronics | 2 years |
| Battery | 2 years or 600 charge cycles, whichever comes first |

During the battery warranty, the battery is guaranteed to keep at least 70% of its
original capacity.

Wear parts are not covered: tyres, brake pads, chain, cassette and grips. Damage from
accidents, racing or unauthorised modifications (for example speed tuning) is not
covered either.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Find the serial number, engraved under the bottom bracket.
2. Open a claim on the support portal with the serial number, a photo of the problem
   and your proof of purchase.
3. The support team answers within 3 business days.
4. If the repair is accepted, we send a free shipping label or book a visit to a
   partner workshop near you.
