# Nordlys E1 — User Manual

The Nordlys E1 is a city e-bike designed for daily commuting.

## Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 250 W rear hub motor, 65 Nm torque |
| Battery | 540 Wh, removable, lockable |
| Range | 50 to 120 km depending on assist mode, load and terrain |
| Charging time | 0–80% in 3 hours, 0–100% in 5 hours |
| Assisted top speed | 25 km/h |
| Weight | 22.5 kg with battery |
| Maximum load | 130 kg (rider + luggage) |

## Assist modes

- **Eco**: longest range, gentle assistance.
- **Tour**: balanced, recommended for daily use.
- **Boost**: maximum assistance for hills, shortest range.
- **Walk mode**: hold the "−" button for 2 seconds to push the bike at 6 km/h.

## Display error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Battery communication error | Remove the battery and put it back until it clicks. |
| E02 | Speed sensor error | Check that the magnet on the rear wheel is in place and close to the sensor. |
| E03 | Controller fault | Do not ride. Contact Nordlys service. |
| E04 | Motor overheating | Stop riding and let the motor cool down for 15 minutes. |
| E05 | Battery temperature out of range | Charge only between 0 °C and 40 °C. Bring the battery inside. |
| E07 | Firmware mismatch | Update the bike from the Nordlys Ride app. |

## Maintenance

- Check tyre pressure every week: 3.5 to 5 bar.
- Lubricate the chain every 300 km, or after riding in the rain.
- Have the bike serviced every 1,500 km or once a year, whichever comes first.

## Battery care

- In winter, store the battery indoors at 30–60% charge.
- Do not leave the battery fully discharged for more than 2 weeks.
- Use only the original 4 A charger.
