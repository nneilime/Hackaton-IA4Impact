# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where do you deliver?

We deliver to all EU countries, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway.
Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days. The bike arrives 95% assembled: you only need to
attach the handlebar and the pedals (tools included).

## How much does it cost?

- Nordlys E1: €2,490
- Nordlys E1 Cargo: €3,290
- Spare battery (540 Wh): €549
- Child seat for the E1 Cargo: €179

## Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. We offer 0% financing over 12 months, or 4.9% over 24 or 36 months, subject to
approval by our financing partner.

## Can I try a bike before buying?

Yes. Book a free 30-minute test ride online in one of our showrooms in Oslo, Berlin or
Lyon. Showrooms are open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00.

## Is there an app?

Yes, "Nordlys Ride" for iOS and Android. It shows battery level and range, records
your rides, updates the bike firmware and can lock the motor when the bike is parked.

## Do I need a licence or insurance?

No. The E1 assists up to 25 km/h with a 250 W motor, so it is legally a bicycle in the
EU, the UK, Switzerland and Norway. We still recommend theft insurance.
